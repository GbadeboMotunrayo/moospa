## 🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM AT A GLANCE

### Brand Colors
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Jet Black | #0d0d0d | Primary backgrounds |
| Charcoal | #1a1a1a | Cards, panels |
| Hot Pink | #e91e8c | Primary accent, CTAs |
| Dark Pink | #c71873 | Hover states |
| Gold | #d4af37 | Luxury accents |
| Ivory | #f5f1e8 | Light text |
| Success Green | #2e7d32 | In Stock |
| Warning Yellow | #f57f17 | Low Stock |
| Error Red | #c62828 | Out of Stock |

### Typography
- **Headers**: Georgia or Playfair Display (serif)
- **Body**: Segoe UI or Inter (sans-serif)
- **Sizes**: H1 48-64px, H2 36-48px, Body 14-16px
