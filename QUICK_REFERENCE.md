## 🎬 DESIGN COMPONENTS

### Button Styles
**Primary CTA**:
- Background: #e91e8c
- Text: White, bold, uppercase, 14px
- Padding: 14px 28px
- Hover: #c71873 + slight scale

**Secondary Button**:
- Border: 2px #e91e8c
- Text: #e91e8c
- Hover: Fill with pink

### Product Card Hover
- Image: Slight zoom (1.05)
- Shadow: Enhanced depth
- Price: Highlight in pink
- CTA: Fade in smoothly

### Navigation
- Dark background (#1a1a1a)
- Light text with pink hover
- Sticky on scroll (semi-transparent)
- Mobile: Elegant hamburger menu

## 💼 CONTENT STRUCTURE

### Homepage Sections
1. Hero (image + tagline + CTA)
2. Featured products (3-4 bestsellers)
3. Categories (4 main categories)
4. Brand story (150-200 words)
5. Testimonials (3-4 reviews)
6. Newsletter signup
7. Trust badges/certifications

### Product Pages
1. Image gallery (multiple angles)
2. Product info (name, price, category)
3. Description (benefits, features)
4. Ingredients/specs
5. Customer reviews
6. Related products
7. Add to cart/wishlist CTAs

## 🎁 DESIGN DIFFERENTIATORS

1. **Dark Luxury**: Not black, but #0d0d0d (slightly warm)
2. **Sensual Pink**: Specific shade #e91e8c (vibrant, not pale)
3. **Serif Headers**: Georgia/Playfair (timeless elegance)
4. **High-Quality Photos**: Professional, not stock
5. **Diverse Models**: Real representation, authentic
6. **Smooth Animations**: 0.3-0.4s, purposeful
7. **Premium Spacing**: Breathable, not cramped
8. **Clear CTAs**: Pink buttons, obvious next actions
